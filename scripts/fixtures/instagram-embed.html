Lunches That Keep, Episode 2: Chicken Caesar Baked Wraps 🥙

Creamy, crunchy and great cold. Full method is in the comments!

Ingredients (Serves 3–4)
* 500g cooked chicken, shredded
* 4 large tortillas
* 1 romaine heart, chopped
* 5 tbsp mixed seeds
Lighter Caesar dressing:
* 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard
* ½ tsp garlic granules
* Splash of water to loosen

Method
Mix the dressing ingredients and loosen with water.
Toss the chicken, lettuce and seeds with the dressing, then roll up in the tortillas.
Bake at 200C for 12 minutes until golden.

Full written recipe: https://recipes.example.com/caesar-wraps
