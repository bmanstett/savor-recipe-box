Maple Pecan Granola

Big clusters, not too sweet.

Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 3 cups rolled oats
  • 1 cup pecans, roughly chopped
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ¼ cup coconut oil, melted

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 300°F and line a sheet pan.
  2. Mix everything, spread it out, and bake for 35 minutes without stirring.
  3. Cool completely before breaking into clusters.
