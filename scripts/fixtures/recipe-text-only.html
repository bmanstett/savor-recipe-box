Grandma’s Crunchy Lemon Parmesan Salad

This is the salad everyone asks about. There are just a few things to know about making it.

Ingredients

1 head romaine lettuce, chopped
1/2 cup grated parmesan
1 lemon, juiced
3 tbsp olive oil
1 cup crunchy croutons
Salt and pepper to taste

How To Make Grandma’s Salad

Toss the lettuce with the lemon juice and olive oil.
Add the parmesan and croutons and toss again.
Season with salt and pepper and serve right away.
