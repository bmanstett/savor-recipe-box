Garlic Bread with Herb Butter

5 from 12 votes

Brown it. That is the secret to this bread, and it comes together in 20 minutes.

Share Pin recipe

Ingredients (Serves 4):

  1. 1 loaf bread
  2. 2 tbsp butter
  3. 2 cloves garlic, minced
  4. Fresh parsley for garnish

Procedure

Step 1: Preheat the oven to 400°F.

Step 2: Spread the herb butter over the bread.

3. Bake for 10 minutes until golden.

Notes

Store leftovers wrapped in foil for up to 2 days.

Nutrition

Calories: 320, Fat: 12g, Carbohydrates: 40g
