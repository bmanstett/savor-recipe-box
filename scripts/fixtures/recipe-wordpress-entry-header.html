Pot Roast

By Fixture Cook · 5 from 12 votes · Jump to Recipe · Print Recipe

This is the pot roast my family asks for every Sunday.

FacebookPinterestShare on X

Ingredients

  • 1 chuck roast (about 3 pounds)
  • 2 onions, quartered
  • 4 carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Brown the roast on all sides.
  2. Add the onions, carrots and broth.
  3. Cover and braise for 3 hours.
  4. Serve immediately.

Notes

Leftovers keep for 3 days.
