Weeknight Lemon Orzo

One pot, twenty minutes, very lemony.

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup orzo
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • ½ cup grated parmesan

Instructions

  1. Toast the orzo in a dry pot for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the broth and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring often.
  3. Stir in the lemon and parmesan and serve.
