Crispy Smashed Potatoes

Boiled, smashed and roasted until shatteringly crisp.
4 10 minutes 45 minutes

Potatoes

  • 2 lb baby potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil

Garlic butter

  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, about 15 minutes.
  • Smash each potato on an oiled sheet pan and brush with garlic butter.
  • Roast at 450°F for 30 minutes until crisp.
